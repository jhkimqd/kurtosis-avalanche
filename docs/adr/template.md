# NNNN. Title: the decision as a short statement

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN

## Context

The situation and the forces that require a decision. Name the files, the upstream behaviour, or the measurements that matter.

## Decision

What we do, in the active voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what a contributor must now do or must not do.

## Alternatives

Each option that we rejected, with the reason in one sentence.
